# Video reference — what the site must look and feel like

Source: a 16-second phone recording of a laptop scrolling through the demo site
"Northlight Painting Co. — Interior & Exterior Painters" (browser tab title).
Twelve stills from the recording are saved in [`reference/`](reference/) for comparison.

## Overall style
- Quiet, editorial, premium. Lots of white space, thin serif headlines, small sans text.
- Palette: warm off-white page, soft grey "wall" in the hero, one paint colour — a muted
  leafy green — used for the paint stripe, icons, testimonial band and the main button.
- Type: light high-contrast serif for headlines (we use **Newsreader**), with an italic
  word in most headlines; small uppercase letter-spaced eyebrow labels; **Inter** for body text.
- Rounded corners (~14px) on cards, images and the form card; pill-shaped buttons.

## Header
- Left: small green dot + "Northlight" (serif).
- Right: Services · Process · Work · outlined pill "Get a quote".
- Transparent with white text over the hero; solid off-white with a bottom hairline afterwards.

## Hero animation details
- Section is pinned (sticky) while the user scrolls ~4 screens.
- Roller is drawn front-on: horizontal green cover, silver wire frame from the right end
  bending to the centre, black handle pointing down. Soft shadow on the wall.
- Stripe width ≈ roller cover width (~17% of the viewport on desktop), centred.
- Stripe colour: darker green at the top, lighter toward the bottom; edges are ragged.
- Headlines are white with a soft shadow, centred; the italic word is a pale green-white.
