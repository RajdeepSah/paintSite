How it goes

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We visit, measure, and listen. You get a fixed written quote — no vague hourly games.

  2. 02

    Prep

    Patch, sand, mask and prime. The boring part nobody sees is where the finish is won.

  3. 03

    Paint

    Cut-ins by hand, even rolls, two full coats. We photograph progress daily.

  4. 04

    Walk & clean

    We inspect together under the room's own light, touch up, and leave it spotless.
